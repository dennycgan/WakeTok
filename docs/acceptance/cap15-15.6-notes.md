## Inferred PhotoKit behavior model (not Apple-documented)

**Inference**, not a cited public spec. Apple does not document that Limited
and Full use different opportunistic fallback tiers for
`PHImageManager.requestImage`.

| Authorization | Observed delivery under our production options | Miss under local-only |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Full** | First completing opportunistic result is consistently a **~120** system-thumb class image for the Protocol B set (and Session A feed) | ≈0 when that tier exists |
| **Limited** | Completing results are **512-edge** when a local derivative at/near the request box exists; otherwise **miss** (`inCloud=true` in our logs) | Residual miss when no usable local derivative at that tier |

Model in one line: Full appears to fall back to a **fixed small local thumb
tier**; Limited appears to **search by `targetSize`** without that same
always-available fallback for these assets.

### Caveats (do not over-claim)

1. **First-delivery completion:** Host `requestPreview` accepts the first
   opportunistic image (`takeForDelivery`) and cancels further upgrades. “Full
   never larger than ~120” in our logs is **what Layer 1 stores**, not a proof
   that PhotoKit would never deliver a larger buffer later under Full.
2. **Optimize Storage “immunity”:** We did **not** toggle Optimize Storage in
   Protocol B. “Always retained / immune to eviction” is a **plausible
   explanation** for why Full had ~120 when Limited missed — not a separately
   measured property.
3. **512 vs 1080 under Full:** Not A/B’d on these 16 ids with a 1080 box.
   Indifference of Full miss-rate to box size is an **inference** from the
   tier model + Session A morphology, not a second controlled experiment.
4. **`.fastFormat`:** Unvalidated. May or may not surface the small thumb
   tier under Limited. Requires a new same-id Full/Limited × deliveryMode
   matrix before product claims.

## Meaning for the app

| Mode | Layer 1 under local-only | Role of `previewTargetSize` 512 |
|------|---------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Full** | Miss rate ≈0 on exercised paths; **resolution ceiling for stored L1 ≈ ~120** with current completion policy | Largely **out of the competition** for miss-rate; Full “never black” is **not** explained by 512 |
| **Limited** | Hit rate improves when the box matches available local derivatives better (1080→512; 15.1 context + Session B 13×512-edge hits) | **Real lever** for debt 400–class Limited poster misses |
| **Limited residual** | Some assets still miss at 512 (Protocol B: 3 ids that Full can serve at ~120) | **Not** fixable by further Host box shrink alone under ADR-025 local-only; PhotoKit Limited delivery behavior |

App Host code is **not** forking Full vs Limited in `requestPreview`. The
asymmetry is PhotoKit’s observed behavior under the same call site.

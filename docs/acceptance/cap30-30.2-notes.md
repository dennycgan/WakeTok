## Accepted risk — mid-chain ready flip

Mid-chain `browseItemReady` flipping **true→false** is **out of scope /
accepted risk** for Cap 30.2. The orchestrator does **not** cancel, pause, or
roll back the step index when ready drops. Documented on the orchestrator
class and here so it is not an unspoken gap.

## Future clips step (D8 note)

appends a clips tip must migrate / reinterpret stored `3` (“v1 chain
finished”) — do not assume it means “all future tips finished”.

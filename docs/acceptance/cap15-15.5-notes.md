## Reading precision / ascending-peak caveat (mandatory)

S1 (~219) and S2 (~243) are **owner estimates** from screenshots that
missed the absolute crest; only S3 (261) was captured near peak.
Precision is **not uniform** across the three readings.

**Ascending 219 → 243 → 261 is plausible** (cumulative decode / retain
pressure across consecutive stills) but **not established as a stable
law** from one n=3 session. Do **not** treat the monotonic rise as proven
cache physics.

Therefore:

1. Using **261** as `still_handoff_peak` (max of observed still-window
   readings) is the agreed representative value.
2. Because S1/S2 may be **under-read**, the true still-window max may be
   **higher than 261**. If after vs before sits in a **borderline** rise,
   **prefer suspecting before underestimation** over declaring a gapless
   memory regression — unless multi-run variance or Allocations diagnosis
   supports a real regression.
